## Why the New Holland 3630 4x4 Price in India is Justified for Telangana Soils

Farmers in Nizamabad, Armoor, and Bodhan face unique soil challenges. Our region features a mix of rich, sticky black cotton soil and heavy clay-loam fields. While a standard two-wheel-drive (2WD) tractor often struggles with tyre slippage during wet paddy puddling or deep sub-soiling, the four-wheel-drive (4WD) variant of the New Holland 3630 TX Super Plus handles these conditions effortlessly.

When you invest in the 4WD version, you are not just paying for front-wheel traction. You are buying an advanced front axle system that distributes power to all four tyres. This distribution reduces wheel slippage by up to 25% compared to a 2WD tractor. In practical terms, this means your rotavator operations in the wet paddy fields of Banswada or Kamareddy are completed much faster, saving you valuable diesel and time during tight sowing windows.

Additionally, the front heavy-duty axle adds substantial weight to the front end. This extra weight prevents front-end lifting when pulling heavy implements like reversible moldboard ploughs or laser land levelers, making your field operations safer and more stable.

## 2WD vs 4WD Math: Is the Extra Cost Worth It?

Many buyers visiting our Nizamabad showroom ask if the price difference of roughly Rs 1.5 lakh between the 2WD and 4WD models of the 3630 is worth the investment. Let us look at the actual numbers over a year of typical operation:

1. **Diesel Efficiency:** A 4WD tractor experiences significantly less wheel slip. While a 2WD tractor might slip 15-20% in wet clay, the 4x4 keeps slippage below 5%. This directly translates to a saving of 1 to 1.5 litres of diesel per hour of heavy puddling.
2. **Tyre Longevity:** Rear tyres on a 2WD tractor wear out quickly when subjected to constant slippage on hard soils or wet mud. By distributing the tractive force across all four wheels, the 3630 4x4 extends the overall life of your rear tyres, delaying expensive replacements.
3. **Labor Savings:** Because the 4WD version covers more ground per hour, you complete your primary tillage and puddling faster. This allows you to rent your tractor out to neighboring farms in Kamareddy or Jagtial during peak seasons, generating extra income.

If your farm operations are limited to dry-land haulage of sugarcane or light trolley work, a 2WD model will serve you well. However, if you run heavy rotavators, subsoilers, or operate in deep muddy paddy fields, the 4WD model pays for its price premium within two to three seasons of operation.

## Key Technical Specifications of the 55 HP New Holland 3630 4x4

The reason the new holland 3630 4x4 price in india commands a premium is its robust, heavy-duty build. Here is what you get under the hood:

* **FPT Engine Technology:** The heart of this machine is a 3-cylinder Fiat Power Train (FPT) engine. It is highly regarded across Telangana for its high torque backup, meaning the tractor does not choke or drop RPM even when hitting sudden patches of hard clay.
* **Double Clutch with Independent PTO:** This is crucial for rotavator and thresher operations. The independent PTO lever allows you to engage or disengage the PTO without stopping the tractor's forward motion, preventing blockages and saving clutch wear.
* **Fully Constant Mesh Gearbox:** Offers smooth gear shifts with 8 Forward + 2 Reverse or optional 12 Forward + 3 Reverse (with Creeper speeds), giving you the exact speed match for any implement.
* **Real-time 4WD Engagement:** Easily switch between 2WD and 4WD using a robust mechanical lever located next to the driver's seat. Use 2WD on tarmac roads to save fuel and tyres, and engage 4WD the moment you enter the field.
