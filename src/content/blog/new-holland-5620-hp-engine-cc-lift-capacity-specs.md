## Transmission and PTO

This robust tractor typically comes with a fully constant mesh or synchromesh gearbox, offering a good selection of speeds for various field conditions and transport. The independent power take-off (PTO) with multiple speeds (standard and reverse PTO options often available) ensures compatibility with a wide range of PTO-driven implements, from rotavators to threshers. The ability to select the right PTO speed helps in optimizing fuel consumption and improving implement performance.
