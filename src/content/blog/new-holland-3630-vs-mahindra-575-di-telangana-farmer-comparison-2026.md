## Engine Performance and Paddy Farming Usability

In Telangana, tractor performance is judged by how well it handles a rotavator in a flooded paddy field. The New Holland 3630 comes with a 3-cylinder, 2960 CC engine. It delivers high torque backup, meaning the tractor doesn't stall easily when the rotavator hits a hard patch of clay in the fields around Kamareddy or Jagtial.

The Mahindra 575 DI has a 2730 CC engine. It is fuel-efficient and reliable for general haulage and standard tilling, but when you hook up a heavy MB plough or a large rotavator, the 47 HP rating shows its limits. You will frequently need to downshift or use the lower gears to maintain momentum, which consumes more fuel over time.

### Hydraulic Lifting Capacity

That 300 kg difference means you can carry heavier implements without straining the tractor's hydraulics, directly translating to faster field coverage and less wear and tear on the machine.
