## What Factors Determine the On-Road Price in Nizamabad?

When you ask about the **new holland 3630 price**, the number you see on brochures is always the ex-showroom price. The actual money you pay to drive the tractor home to your farm is the "on-road price." This is calculated by adding several local costs to the base price:

1.  **RTO Registration and Road Tax:** The government of Telangana charges a registration fee and road tax. This fee differs depending on whether you register the tractor for agricultural use or commercial haulage (such as transporting sand, bricks, or construction materials in Kamareddy).
2.  **Insurance Premium:** Tractors are heavy machinery. Having comprehensive insurance is essential to cover third-party liabilities, accidental damage, and natural disasters. The premium depends on the IDV (Insured Declared Value) of the specific 3630 variant you choose.
3.  **Local Dealer Charges & Accessories:** This includes essential additions like heavy front bumpers, canopy structures for sun protection, hitch assemblies, and drawbars.
4.  **Government Subsidies:** The Telangana state farm mechanization department occasionally offers subsidies on tractors and matching implements. To understand the current status of these schemes and how they can lower your overall investment, read our [/blog/telangana-tractor-subsidy-complete-guide-2026](/blog/telangana-tractor-subsidy-complete-guide-2026).

## Why Buy the New Holland 3630 Over Competitors?

Farmers in Telangana often compare the New Holland 3630 with other popular models in the market, such as the Mahindra 575 DI or Arjun, Swaraj 744, John Deere 5050D, and Sonalika models. While those are respectable machines, the 3630 stands apart due to its unique engineering advantages:

*   **Double Clutch with Independent PTO (IPTO):** Unlike traditional tractors where the PTO stops when you press the main clutch, New Holland's independent PTO lever allows you to control the tractor's movement and the implement independently. This is highly useful when operating rotavators or straw balers in dense paddy fields.
*   **Real Oil-Immersed Multi-Disc Brakes (OIB):** These brakes offer superior stopping power, minimal wear, and long life, even when hauling massive sugarcane loads down the slopes of Armoor.
*   **High Lift Capacity:** With a robust hydraulic lift capacity of 1500 to 2000 kg, the 3630 handles modern, heavy agricultural implements without any hydraulic strain.
