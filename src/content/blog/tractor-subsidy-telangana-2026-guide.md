The reality of the **tractor subsidy Telangana** scheme as of July 2026 is that the state government focuses heavily on the Rythu Bandhu scheme for crop investment, while farm mechanization is supported through the Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana (RKVY) and SMAM (Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization).

If you are expecting a flat 40% discount on a 55 HP tractor at the dealership, that is not how the current system works. The Telangana government provides a 50% subsidy (capped at Rs 50,000) primarily on agricultural implements. For tractors, direct subsidies are heavily restricted and generally only apply to specific categories of farmers (like SC/ST communities or small/marginal farmers) for tractors below 35 HP.

## Eligibility Criteria for Telangana Farm Mechanization Subsidy

To qualify for the tractor and implement subsidy in 2026, you must meet these baseline requirements set by the Telangana State Government:

* **Land Ownership:** You must own agricultural land in Telangana and possess an updated Land Passbook (Pahani).
* **Farmer Category:** General category farmers are eligible for implement subsidies. For tractor subsidies, priority is given to Small and Marginal Farmers (SMF), Scheduled Castes (SC), and Scheduled Tribes (ST).
* **Previous Subsidy:** You should not have received a tractor subsidy from the government in the past 7 years.
* **Local Residency:** Must be a resident of Telangana with valid local proof.

### Step 2: Online Application via Agrimachinery Portal
Visit the official Telangana agrimachinery subsidy portal. Register as a farmer using your Aadhaar and mobile number. Fill out the application, upload your documents, and attach the dealer quotation.

### Step 3: DAO Verification
After online submission, take a printout of the application and submit it to your local District Agriculture Officer (DAO) or Mandal Agriculture Officer (MAO). They will verify your land details and forward the file to the District Level Committee.

### Step 4: Approval and Purchase
Once approved, the subsidy amount will be directly credited to the dealer's account (Direct Benefit Transfer), meaning you only pay the net amount upfront.
