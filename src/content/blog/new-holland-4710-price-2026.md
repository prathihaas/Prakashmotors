## Best Use-Cases for New Holland Excel 4710

### 1. Paddy Transplanting & Harvesting

The 4710's low-end torque (peak at 1600 RPM) makes it perfect for paddy fields in Nizamabad district — Bodhan, Banswada, and Armoor. It can pull a 6-row transplanter without bogging down in wet clay.

### 2. Cotton Cultivation

Cotton farmers in Kamareddy and Nirmal prefer this model for inter-row operations. The narrow front hood gives good visibility for spraying and weeding. With a 12+12 shuttle shift, turning at headlands is quick.

### 3. Horticulture & Orchard Work

If you grow mango, chilli, or turmeric, the 4710's 47 HP is enough for a 1.5-tonne trailer. The power steering (standard) reduces fatigue during long hauls to Nizamabad market.

### 4. Rental / Custom Hiring

Many small farmers in Karimnagar use the 4710 for rental rotavation at Rs 800–1200 per hour. The 3-point lift capacity (1800 kg) handles heavy implements.

## New Holland 4710 vs 3600-2 Excel – Which to Choose?

Many buyers ask: should I spend extra for the 3600-2 Excel (49 HP, Rs 7.9–8.5 lakh) or stick with the 4710? Here's our honest take:

- **If you do heavy rotavation** in black cotton soil (common in Nizamabad), the 3600-2 Excel's extra 2 HP and heavier chassis (2150 kg vs 2050 kg) give better traction.
- **If you prioritize fuel economy** and mostly do transplanting, spraying, and light trailer work, the 4710 is more efficient — you'll save Rs 30–40 per hour on diesel.
