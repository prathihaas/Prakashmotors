## What is a KCC Tractor Loan?

The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme, run by NABARD through banks, was originally for crop loans. But since 2019, it also covers investment credit for agriculture — including tractor purchase. In Telangana, most nationalised banks (SBI, Canara, Andhra Bank, Union Bank) and cooperative banks offer KCC loans for tractors. The loan is a flexible credit line: you can withdraw up to your sanctioned limit, repay, and reuse. For a tractor, the bank typically sanctions a term loan component under KCC.

That's lower than any tractor loan from NBFCs or private banks.

## KCC Tractor Loan Eligibility in Telangana

To get a KCC loan for a tractor, you must:
- Be a farmer (individual, joint, or group) engaged in agriculture.
- Have land records (pattadar passbook / 1B extract) in your name or family.
- Not have defaulted on any previous agricultural loan.
- Be between 18 and 70 years old.
- For tractor purchase, the loan is typically up to ₹3 lakh. If you need more, you can take a joint KCC with a family member or top up with a separate tractor loan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### KCC tractor loan eligibility Telangana: who can apply?
Any farmer in Telangana with land records and a valid Aadhaar can apply. Farmers from Nizamabad, Kamareddy, Jagtial, Karimnagar, and surrounding mandals like Armoor, Banswada, Bodhan, and Bhainsa are eligible.

### How much KCC loan can I get for a tractor?
The standard limit is ₹3 lakh for individual farmers. Joint account with spouse or family member can increase the limit. Some banks offer up to ₹5 lakh for group farming.

### Can I use KCC loan for second-hand tractor?
No, KCC loan for tractor purchase is only for new tractors. For second-hand, you need a separate loan product.

### What is the repayment period for KCC tractor loan?
Typically 3-5 years. You can repay in annual installments or flexible EMI. Early repayment has no penalty.
