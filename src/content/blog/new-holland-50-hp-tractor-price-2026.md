## On-Road Price and What Influences It

The ex-showroom price is just the starting point. The final **New Holland tractor 50 HP on-road price** will include several additional components:

1.  **RTO Registration Charges:** These are mandatory government charges for vehicle registration and vary by state. For Telangana, these are fixed percentages based on the tractor's ex-showroom price.
2.  **Insurance:** Comprehensive insurance is crucial and its cost depends on the insured declared value (IDV) of the tractor and the chosen policy.
3.  **Road Tax:** Another state-specific tax that contributes to the final cost.
4.  **Accessories:** Optional accessories like bumpers, drawbar, top link, and canopy will add to the price.
5.  **Dealer Handling Charges:** Minor charges for logistics and preparation.
