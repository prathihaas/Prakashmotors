When planning your budget, remember that the ex-showroom price is only the starting point. The final **on-road price** you pay to drive the tractor home to your village involves several mandatory and optional costs:

1. **Telangana RTO Registration Fees:** This includes life tax and registration charges. For agricultural tractors, Telangana charges a lower road tax compared to commercial registrations, but the exact amount varies slightly between RTO offices in Nizamabad (TS-16), Kamareddy (TS-17), Jagtial (TS-21), and Karimnagar (TS-02).
2. **Comprehensive Tractor Insurance:** Protecting your investment is non-negotiable. Agricultural tractor insurance covers third-party liabilities, personal accident cover for the driver, and own-damage protection against fire, theft, or natural disasters.
3. **Dealer Accessories & Hitch/Drawbar:** Standard accessories like front bumper weights, canopy, heavy-duty drawbars, and rear hitch are essential for safe field operations.
