### Why the Price Varies: Understanding On-Road Costs in Telangana

The ex-showroom price is just the base. For the final on-road cost, several factors come into play:

1.  **RTO Registration Charges:** This is a significant component and varies based on the vehicle type and district. For instance, registering a machine in Nizamabad might have slightly different charges than in Kamareddy or Jagtial. This fee covers the vehicle's registration, number plates, and other administrative costs.
2.  **Insurance:** Comprehensive tractor insurance is mandatory and its cost depends on the insured declared value (IDV) of the tractor, the insurer you choose, and the type of coverage. We can help you compare options.
3.  **Road Tax:** While often included in RTO, sometimes there's a separate road tax component for commercial vehicles like tractors.
4.  **Accessories:** Any additional attachments like front loaders, rotavators, cultivators, or even basic accessories like bumpers or canopies will add to the total cost. Many farmers prefer to buy these from us directly, ensuring compatibility and quality.
5.  **Logistics/Handling Charges:** While minimal, some dealers might include a nominal charge for transportation from the factory to the showroom.

At our Nizamabad showroom, the question we hear most often is about the difference between ex-showroom and on-road. We take the time to break down each component, ensuring complete transparency. It’s important to see the full picture before making a decision.
