## Rythu Bharosa 2026: Eligibility and How It Works

At our Nizamabad showroom, the question we hear most is whether the benefit can be combined with the Telangana farm mechanization subsidy for implements. Yes – the cash transfer can be used for any approved agricultural input, while the mechanization subsidy applies separately to eligible implements such as rotavators, seed drills or trailers. This combination often makes the difference between postponing a purchase and buying this season.

Eligibility targets small and marginal farmers; verification is based on landholding size through the Telangana agriculture portal. Once approved, the amount is transferred directly to the beneficiary’s bank account, typically in two installments. There is no restriction on using the funds for a tractor down payment, provided the purchase serves farming purposes.

Application is straightforward: farmers log in to the Rythu Bharosa website with their Aadhaar‑linked mobile number, upload land records, and submit the form. Alternatively, a visit to the nearest MeeSeva center in towns like Bodhan or Kamareddy yields same‑day assistance. Our sales team often helps customers print the acknowledgment slip, which we then treat as part of the down‑payment proof when arranging finance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the eligibility for Rythu Bharosa 2026?

Eligibility is limited to small and marginal farmers who own up to 2.5 acres of agricultural land, as per the latest Telangana government notification. The benefit is granted after verification of land records through the agriculture department’s portal. Farmers outside this land‑size bracket should contact us for alternative subsidy options that may apply.

### How can I apply the Rythu Bharosa amount toward a tractor purchase?

Once the benefit is credited to your bank account, you can use those funds as part of the down payment when financing a tractor through our KCC‑linked loan scheme. There is no requirement to purchase specific implements; the cash can be allocated to any farming‑related expense, including the tractor itself.

### Do I need to separate the Rythu Bharosa benefit from the Telangana farm mechanization subsidy?

No. The two schemes are independent. The cash transfer can be used for any agricultural input, while the mechanization subsidy applies to eligible implements such as rotavators, seed drills or trailers. Many of our customers combine both to reduce the overall outlay for a tractor‑implement package.

### What documents do I need to show at Prakash Motors to avail the benefit?

Bring your Aadhaar card, land ownership documents ( Pahani or 1‑B ), and the Rythu Bharosa acknowledgment slip or bank statement showing the credit. Our finance team will verify the details and incorporate the amount into your down‑payment calculation.
