### What Affects the New Holland 5630 On-Road Price?

The on-road price is what you actually pay to drive the tractor out of our showroom and onto your fields. For this powerful machine, this will include:

1.  **RTO (Regional Transport Office) Registration:** This is a mandatory charge for registering the vehicle with the government. The amount varies based on the district and the tractor's ex-showroom price. In Nizamabad, these charges are standard, but they can differ slightly if you're registering in, say, Kamareddy or Jagtial.
2.  **Insurance:** Comprehensive insurance is essential to protect your investment. The premium depends on the insured declared value (IDV) of the tractor and the chosen insurance provider.
3.  **Road Tax:** While not always a separate line item for agricultural tractors in the same way as cars, some state-specific levies may apply.
4.  **Accessories (Optional):** Any additional attachments or accessories you choose, like a bumper, canopy, or specific drawbar hitches, will add to the total cost.
5.  **Logistics/Handling Charges:** Minor charges may be included for transportation from the factory to the dealership.

Given these variables, it's impossible to give a single, exact on-road price here. We strongly recommend contacting us directly for a precise, up-to-the-minute quote based on your specific location and requirements. This is especially true as RTO charges and insurance premiums can fluctuate.
