### ## What is the On-Road Price of a 55 HP New Holland Tractor in Nizamabad?

The on-road price is what you actually pay to drive your new tractor out of the showroom. It includes the ex-showroom price plus several mandatory charges:

1.  **RTO (Regional Transport Office) Registration:** This is a state-specific tax and fee for registering your vehicle. In Telangana, RTO charges can vary based on the tractor's value and type, typically a percentage of the ex-showroom price. For a 55 HP tractor, expect this to add a significant amount to the final cost.
2.  **Insurance:** Comprehensive tractor insurance is mandatory. The premium depends on the tractor's value, the chosen insurance provider, and the type of coverage. We at Prakash Motors can help you get competitive quotes.
3.  **Road Tax (if applicable):** While agricultural tractors often have exemptions or lower road tax, it's a component to consider.
4.  **Accessories (Optional but common):** While not mandatory, many farmers opt for additional accessories like bumper, canopy, drawbar, and extra weights, which add to the overall cost. We can discuss popular choices among Nizamabad farmers.
