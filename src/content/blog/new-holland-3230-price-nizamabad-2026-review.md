### New Holland 3230 On-Road Price Breakdown (Nizamabad, August 2026)

Getting a clear picture of this tractor's price requires understanding its components. The ex-showroom price is what the manufacturer charges, but the on-road price, which is what you actually pay, includes several other mandatory charges. Here's a general breakdown:

*   **Ex-showroom Price:** This is the base cost of the tractor. For the 3230, it typically ranges from Rs 6.80 lakh to Rs 7.30 lakh, depending on the specific variant (e.g., with or without power steering, different tire options).
*   **RTO Registration:** The Regional Transport Office charges for vehicle registration. In Telangana, these charges are a percentage of the tractor's cost and can vary slightly. This is a significant part of the on-road price.
*   **Insurance:** Comprehensive insurance is mandatory. The premium depends on the tractor's value and the chosen coverage, usually for a one-year period initially.
*   **Road Tax:** Part of the RTO process, this is a state-specific tax.
*   **Dealer Handling Charges/Logistics:** Minor charges for transporting the tractor from the factory to our showroom and preparing it for delivery.
