### Factors Influencing Combine Harvester Price

Several factors contribute to the final cost of a combine harvester:

*   **Model and Specifications:** Engine HP, cutting width, grain tank capacity, and features like self-leveling systems all impact the price.
*   **Attachments/Headers:** Specific headers for paddy, wheat, maize, or other crops might be included or purchased separately, affecting the overall investment.
*   **RTO and Insurance:** These are mandatory costs in Telangana. RTO charges depend on the vehicle's value, and insurance premiums vary based on coverage. We can provide exact figures when you contact us for a quote.
*   **Taxes:** GST and other applicable taxes are part of the total price.
*   **Transportation:** While often included for local deliveries, confirm this with your dealer.
*   **Dealer Margins and Offers:** While the base prices are set, special offers or seasonal discounts can sometimes be available. It's always best to inquire about current promotions.
